CONNECTED PLASTIC CRUMPLE & WHEELS

Dense welded body meshes use a connected neighbor spring network, not independent vertex animation. Three bounded structural constraint passes couple mesh-edge neighbors around yielding plastic rest positions. The chassis remains rigid; panels never detach. Local permanent displacement is limited to 85 cm on cars, 52 cm on the armored hauler and 20 cm on the motorcycle; smaller parts have tighter limits.

This is not FEM or a full soft-body collision hull. Damage saves per vehicle as up to 48 compact impact zones plus its wheel mask. The saved impacts deterministically reconstruct the plastic rest shape. Paid repair or free Restart restores all damage; ordinary garage visits and switching vehicles do not.

A hard direct wheel contact above 10.5 m/s inside its radius + 27 cm can detach it. Detached wheels become temporary debris. Missing wheels reduce acceleration, braking, speed and steering. The motorcycle has two actual inline wheels.

Helicopter detection uses horizontal distance ≤150 m, not walls/terrain occlusion. Its capped 37 m/s flight speed allows faster cars to escape. An airborne sighting updates ground police last-seen coordinates and keeps heat alive.

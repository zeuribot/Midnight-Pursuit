MIDNIGHT PURSUIT

GARAGE EXPANSION · Seven rides. Fourteen endless districts.

Speeding above 70 mph near police starts a pursuit. Staying in sight builds wanted stars. Military reinforcements join at four stars, anywhere. Break contact to lose heat.

Thirteen upgrades, five levels each. Upgrades apply to every owned ride. Earn one coin per ten meters; deliberate handbrake drifts earn triple.

Buy once, equip free. Buying equips the ride and repairs it. Your equipped vehicle is the live garage preview behind this panel.

Free cosmetics, saved separately for each vehicle. Paint, accents, rims and glass update the actual vehicle; police keep their own livery.

Garage visits restore full health and nitro for free. Coins, purchases and upgrades survive a wreck.

SETTINGS

Paused. Changes apply immediately. Reset settings also resets keybindings, never your progress.

CONTROLS & KEYBINDINGS

Click a binding, then press a physical key. Rebinding replaces all of that action’s aliases. Duplicates are rejected. Escape can be assigned; use Cancel to leave capture. Ctrl/Alt/Meta shortcuts are reserved. Shift alone is allowed for boost.
